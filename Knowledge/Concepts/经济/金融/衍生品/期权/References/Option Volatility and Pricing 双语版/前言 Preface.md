# 前言 / Preface

[[阅读导航|← 返回阅读导航]] · [[翻译说明与术语表|术语表]]

> [!quote] English 1
> It probably seems strange for an author to wait 20 years to revise a professional publication, especially one that has been continuously in print over the entire period. To those of you who were hoping for at least one revision in the intervening years, I can only offer my apology and the excuse that other obligations prevented me from undertaking such a revision.

一位作者等待了 20 年才修订一本专业著作，这大概显得很奇怪，尤其是这本书在整个期间一直持续再版。对于那些原本希望这 20 年间至少能看到一次修订的读者，我只能表示歉意，并解释说，是其他事务使我一直未能着手完成这次修订。

> [!quote] English 2
> Much has changed in option markets over the last 20 years. Most markets are now fully electronic, and the days of floor trading are clearly numbered. Only in the United States do option-trading floors still exist, and even those are inevitably giving way to electronic trading. Twenty years ago, organized option markets existed only in the major industrialized nations. But as the importance of derivatives as both an investment vehicle and a risk-management tool has become widely recognized, new option markets have opened in countries around the world. Options are now traded not only on traditional products—stocks, interest rates, commodities, and foreign currencies—but also on a bewildering array of new products—real estate, pollution, weather, inflation, and insurance. Many exchanges have also added variations on traditional products—short-term and midcurve options, flex options, options on spreads, and implied and realized volatility contracts.

过去 20 年里，期权市场发生了许多变化。如今，大多数市场已经全面电子化，场内喊价交易的时代显然屈指可数。只有美国仍保留着期权交易大厅，但它们也不可避免地正被电子交易取代。20 年前，有组织的期权市场只存在于主要工业化国家。随着衍生品作为投资工具和风险管理工具的重要性得到广泛认可，世界各国陆续建立了新的期权市场。

如今，期权的标的不仅包括股票、利率、商品和外汇等传统产品，也扩展到了令人眼花缭乱的新领域，例如房地产、污染、天气、通货膨胀和保险。许多交易所还在传统产品基础上推出了各种变体，包括短期期权、中曲线期权（midcurve options）、灵活期权（flex options）、价差期权，以及隐含波动率和实际波动率合约。

> [!quote] English 3
> Not only has there been a dramatic increase in the number of option markets, but the traders in those markets have become increasingly sophisticated. When this text was first published, knowledgeable traders could only be found at firms that traded derivatives professionally—market-making firms, hedge funds, investment banks, and other proprietary trading firms. Now, many retail customers have a level of knowledge equal to that of a professional trader. At the same time, universities are adding or expanding programs in financial engineering. In many cases, those who choose a career in derivatives trading have already had in-depth exposure to the mathematics of option pricing.

不仅期权市场的数量大幅增加，市场参与者也变得越来越成熟。本书第一版出版时，真正掌握专业知识的交易者几乎只存在于专门从事衍生品交易的机构中，例如做市公司、对冲基金、投资银行及其他自营交易机构。如今，许多个人投资者的知识水平已经可以与专业交易者相提并论。与此同时，大学也在新设或扩展金融工程课程。很多选择衍生品交易作为职业的人，在进入行业之前就已经深入接触过期权定价数学。

> [!quote] English 4
> While much has changed in the last 20 years, much has also remained the same. There is still a core body of material that a serious option trader needs to master, and this core material is much the same as it has always been. The previous edition of this text was an attempt to present this material in a manner that was easily accessible and that did not require a familiarity with advanced mathematics. This edition retains that approach. Although some presentations may have been changed in the interest of improving an explanation or clarifying a concept, all the major topics from the previous edition have been retained.

尽管过去 20 年发生了很多变化，也有许多东西并未改变。严肃的期权交易者仍然需要掌握一套核心知识，而这套核心知识与过去相比并没有太大不同。本书上一版试图以易于理解、无须预先熟悉高等数学的方式呈现这些内容，本版仍然保留了这种做法。为了改善说明或澄清概念，部分表达方式有所调整，但上一版的所有主要主题都得到了保留。

> [!quote] English 5
> So what’s new in this edition? As in the first edition, an attempt has been made to explain important concepts in the simplest possible manner using an intuitive rather than mathematical approach. However, it is also true that a full understanding of many option concepts requires a familiarity with more advanced mathematics. Consequently, some explanations have been expanded to include a discussion of the relevant mathematics. But even these discussions tend to avoid mathematical concepts with which many readers are unlikely to be familiar. Many chapters have also been expanded to include a more detailed discussion of the relevant topics. In addition, there are several completely new chapters covering forward pricing, risk dynamics, the Black-Scholes model, binomial option pricing, and volatility contracts.

那么，这一版增加了什么？与第一版一样，本书仍尽可能采用直观方法，而不是纯数学方法，以最简单的方式解释重要概念。不过，要完整理解许多期权概念，确实需要熟悉更高阶的数学。因此，一些说明得到了扩展，并加入了相关数学内容。即便如此，这些讨论仍尽量避开大多数读者可能并不熟悉的数学概念。许多章节也进行了扩充，对相关主题作了更详细的讨论。此外，本版还增加了若干全新章节，涉及远期定价、风险的动态变化、Black–Scholes 模型、二叉树期权定价和波动率合约。

> [!quote] English 6
> As with any living language, market terminology, and more specifically, option terminology, has changed over time. Some terms that were common when the first edition appeared have gone out of favor or disappeared completely. Other terms that did not previously exist have gained wide acceptance. This is reflected in small changes to the vocabulary used in this text.

市场术语——更具体地说，期权术语——与任何活的语言一样，会随着时间变化。第一版问世时常见的一些词语已经不再流行，甚至完全消失；另一些当时尚不存在的词语，如今却已得到广泛接受。本书用词上的一些细微调整正反映了这种变化。

> [!quote] English 7
> It is almost impossible to keep up with the amount of information that is available on options. Not only do new books appear with greater frequency, but the Internet has enabled traders to find relevant source material almost instantaneously. For this reason, the Bibliography has been eliminated. This should not be construed as an attempt to discourage readers from consulting other sources. This book represents only one approach to options—that of a professional trader. Many excellent option books are available, and any aspiring option trader will want to consult a broad range of texts in order to understand the many different ways one can approach option markets. For those who are interested in the mathematics of option pricing, this text is in no way meant to take the place of a good university textbook on financial engineering.

如今，可获得的期权信息数量之大，几乎让人不可能始终跟上。不仅新书出版得越来越频繁，互联网也使交易者几乎可以即时找到相关资料。出于这个原因，本版取消了参考书目。但这绝不意味着我不鼓励读者查阅其他来源。本书只代表理解期权的一种方式——一名专业交易者的方式。

市面上还有许多优秀的期权著作。任何有志于从事期权交易的人，都应该广泛阅读，从而理解人们可以用哪些不同方式研究和参与期权市场。对于对期权定价数学感兴趣的读者，本书绝不打算取代一本优秀的大学金融工程教材。

> [!quote] English 8
> Nothing in this text is really new, and all the concepts will be familiar, in one form or another, to most experienced option traders. The presentation represents my best attempt, as an option educator, to present these concepts in a clear and easily accessible manner. The material is based not only on what I have personally learned throughout my career but also on the knowledge and experiences of many others with whom I have been privileged to work. In particular, my colleagues Tim Weithers and Samuel Kadziela offered many helpful comments and insights and in some cases rescued me from embarrassing errors. Any remaining errors, of which there are almost certainly a few, are strictly my own.

严格来说，本书并没有真正全新的内容；对于大多数经验丰富的期权交易者而言，书中的所有概念都能以某种形式找到熟悉之处。作为一名期权教育者，我已尽最大努力用清晰、易于理解的方式呈现这些概念。

本书内容不仅来自我整个职业生涯中的个人学习，也来自许多有幸与我共事之人的知识和经验。尤其是我的同事 Tim Weithers 和 Samuel Kadziela，他们提供了许多有益的意见与见解，并在一些地方使我避免了令人尴尬的错误。书中如果仍有错误——几乎肯定还有一些——责任完全在我。

> [!quote] English 9
> I make no claim to having found a magic secret to successful option trading. Anyone seeking such a formula will have to look elsewhere. The secret, if there is one, is in learning as much as possible, applying in the real world what has been learned, and analyzing both one’s successes and one’s failures.

我并不声称自己找到了期权交易成功的神奇秘诀。任何希望寻找这种公式的人，都只能去别处寻找。如果真有什么秘诀，那就是尽可能多地学习，把学到的知识应用于现实世界，并同时分析自己的成功与失败。

> [!quote] English 10
> Sheldon Natenberg

Sheldon Natenberg
