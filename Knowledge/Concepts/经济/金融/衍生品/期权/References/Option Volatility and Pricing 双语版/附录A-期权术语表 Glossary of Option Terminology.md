<!-- source:block c7535a8006c436b8 -->

> [!quote]- English 1
> This glossary includes option-related terms as they are most commonly used. However, the reader should be aware that option terminology is not uniform. Traders may sometimes refer to different strategies or option characteristics with the same term. They may sometimes refer to the same strategy or characteristic with different terms.

本术语表包括最常用的与期权相关的术语。然而，读者应该注意，期权术语并不统一。交易员有时可能会用同一术语指代不同的策略或期权特征。它们有时可能会用不同的术语来指代相同的策略或特征。

<!-- source:block dc33e27fe69c0572 -->

> [!quote]- English 2
> **All or None (AON)** An order that must be filled in its entirety or not at all.

全部或无（AON）必须完整填写或根本不填写的订单。

<!-- source:block 711e02f07293a550 -->

> [!quote]- English 3
> **American Option** An option that can be exercised at any time prior to expiration.

美式期权可以在到期前随时行权的期权。

<!-- source:block 5dcd6362e10bb062 -->

> [!quote]- English 4
> **Arbitrage** The purchase and sale of the same or closely related products in different markets to take advantage of a price disparity between the two markets.

套利在不同市场买卖相同或密切相关的产品，以利用两个市场之间的价格差异。

<!-- source:block 81c073fe34b4a75d -->

> [!quote]- English 5
> **Asian Option** See *Average Price Option*.

亚洲期权请参阅平均价格期权。

<!-- source:block cd4bbc3ee0928fd2 -->

> [!quote]- English 6
> **Assignment** The process by which the seller of an option is notified of the buyer’s intention to exercise. The seller is required to take a short position in the underlying position in the case of a call or a long position in the case of a put.

指派期权卖方被告知买方行权期权意图的过程。在看涨的情况下，卖方必须在标的头寸中持有空头头寸，在看跌的情况下，卖方必须在标的头寸中持有多头头寸。

<!-- source:block bdd2d9866375e980 -->

> [!quote]- English 7
> **At the Forward** An option whose exercise price is equal to the forward price of the underlying contract. Sometimes referred to as *At-the-Money Forward*.

远期平值一种期权，其行权价等于标的合约的远期价格。有时称为ATM转发。

<!-- source:block c25d34f9908861e0 -->

> [!quote]- English 8
> **At the Money** An option whose exercise price is equal to the current price of the underlying contract. On listed option exchanges, the term is more commonly used to refer to the option whose exercise price is closest to the current price of the underlying contract.

平值期权一种期权，其行权价等于标的合约的当前价格。在上市期权交易所，该术语更常用于指其行权价最接近标的合约当前价格的期权。

<!-- source:block 22243da600936fba -->

> [!quote]- English 9
> **Automatic Exercise** The exercise by the clearinghouse of an in-the-money option at expiration unless the holder of the option submits specific instructions to the contrary.

自动行权清算所在到期时行权价内期权，除非期权持有人提交相反的具体指示。

<!-- source:block 9e0516902625f7b6 -->

> [!quote]- English 10
> **Average Price Option** An option whose value at expiration is determined by the average price of the underlying instrument over some period of time. Also known as an *Asian Option*.

平均价格期权到期时价值由一段时间内标的工具的平均价格确定的期权。也称为亚洲期权。

<!-- source:block 90c23d7586c487ae -->

> [!quote]- English 11
> **Backspread** A spread, usually delta neutral, where more options are purchased than sold, where all options are the same type and expire at the same time.

Backspread一种价差，通常为Delta中性，即买入期权多于卖出期权，所有期权类型相同，同时到期。

<!-- source:block afc5c511458b2e06 -->

> [!quote]- English 12
> **Backward** A futures market where the long-term delivery months trade at a discount to the short-term delivery months.

反向长期交割月份的交易价格比短期交割月份有折扣的期货市场。

<!-- source:block c5ebe0086976c520 -->

> [!quote]- English 13
> **Barrier Option** A type of exotic option that will either become effective or cease to exist if the underlying instrument trades at or beyond some predetermined price prior to expiration.

障碍期权一种奇异期权，如果标的工具在到期前交易达到或超过某个预定价格，则将生效或停止存在。

<!-- source:block 3c4473ebbf5e22a1 -->

> [!quote]- English 14
> **Bear Spread** Any spread that will theoretically increase in value with a decline in the price of the underlying contract.

熊市价差理论上价值随着标的合约价格下跌而增加的任何价差。

<!-- source:block 76a98d7204024bf2 -->

> [!quote]- English 15
> **Bermuda Option** An option that can be exercised prior to expiration, but only during a predetermined period or window. Also known as a *Mid-Atlantic Option*.

百慕大期权可以在到期前行权的期权，但只能在预定的时期或窗口内行权。也称为大西洋中部期权。

<!-- source:block ee4dd62c041b35a5 -->

> [!quote]- English 16
> **Binary Option** An option that, if in the money at expiration, makes one predetermined payout. Also known as a *Digital Option*.

二元期权如果ITM到期，则进行一次预定付款的期权。也称为数字期权。

<!-- source:block f3abbb1616f1d600 -->

> [!quote]- English 17
> **Box** A long call and short put at one exercise price, together with a short call and long put at a different exercise price. All options must have the same underlying contract and expire at the same time.

方框以一个行权价进行多头看涨和空头看跌，以及以不同行权价进行空头看涨和多头看跌。所有期权必须具有相同的标的合约并同时到期。

<!-- source:block 93179cce490efb7b -->

> [!quote]- English 18
> **Bull Spread** Any spread that will theoretically increase in value with a rise in the price of the underlying contract.

牛市价差理论上价值将随着标的合约价格的上涨而增加的任何价差。

<!-- source:block da6e0e9e9b74a929 -->

> [!quote]- English 19
> **Butterfly** The sale (purchase) of two options with the same exercise price, together with the purchase (sale) of one option with a lower exercise price and one option with a higher exercise price. All options must be of the same type, have the same underlying contract, and expire at the same time, and there must be an equal increment between exercise prices.

蝶式价差出售（购买）具有相同行权价的两个期权，以及购买（出售）一个行权价较低的期权和一个行权价较高的期权。所有期权必须类型相同，具有相同的标的合约，并且同时到期，并且行权价之间必须相等增量。

<!-- source:block 57a592c59f1e57f0 -->

> [!quote]- English 20
> **Buy/Write** The purchase of an underlying contract together with the sale of a call option on that contract.

买入/卖出买入/卖出一份标的合约，同时卖出该合约的看涨期权。

<!-- source:block 517f3073a8255945 -->

> [!quote]- English 21
> **Cabinet Bid** An option price that is smaller than the normally allowable minimum price. On some exchanges, a cabinet bid is permissible between traders desiring to close out positions in very far out-of-the-money options.

内阁出价低于通常允许的最低价格的期权价格。在一些交易所，允许希望平仓深度价外期权头寸的交易员之间进行内阁出价。

<!-- source:block dccfc7826850c994 -->

> [!quote]- English 22
> **Calendar Spread** The purchase (sale) of one option expiring on one date and the sale (purchase) of another option expiring on a different date. Typically, both options are of the same type, have the same exercise price, and have the same underlying stock or commodity. Also known as a *Time Spread* or *Horizontal Spread*.

日历价差购买（出售）在某个日期到期的一个期权以及出售（购买）在不同日期到期的另一个期权。通常，两种期权类型相同，具有相同的行权价，并且具有相同的标的股票或商品。也称为时间价差或水平价差。

<!-- source:block 792ed9c8f28348d2 -->

> [!quote]- English 23
> **Call Option** A contract between a buyer and a seller whereby the buyer acquires the right, but not the obligation, to purchase a specified underlying contract at a fixed price on or before a specified date. The seller of the call option assumes the obligation of delivering the underlying contract should the buyer wish to exercise his option.

看涨期权买方和卖方之间的合约，其中买方获得在指定日期或之前以固定价格购买指定标的合约的权利，但不是义务。如果买方希望行权其选择权，看涨期权的卖方承担交付标的合约的义务。

<!-- source:block f7ee8b3987f2008f -->

> [!quote]- English 24
> **Cap** A contract between a borrower and a lender of floating-rate funds whereby the borrower is assured of paying no more than some maximum interest rate for borrowed funds. This is analogous to a call option where the underlying instrument is an interest rate on borrowed funds.

上限借款人和浮动利率资金贷方之间的合约，借款人保证为借入资金支付不超过某个最高利率。这类似于看涨期权，其中标的工具是借入资金的利率。

<!-- source:block 4435835307b0574b -->

> [!quote]- English 25
> **Charm** The sensitivity of an option’s delta to the passage of time.

Charm期权Delta对时间流逝的敏感性。

<!-- source:block bdc3e6eee33ea840 -->

> [!quote]- English 26
> **Chooser Option** A straddle where the owner must decide by some predetermined date whether to keep either the call or the put.

选择式期权（Chooser Option）：一种跨式期权，其持有人必须在预先确定的日期前，决定保留看涨期权还是看跌期权。

<!-- source:block 43e4e933ebffc302 -->

> [!quote]- English 27
> **Christmas Tree** A spread involving three exercise prices. One or more calls (puts) are purchased at the lowest (highest) exercise price, and one or more calls (puts) are sold at each of the higher (lower) exercise prices. All options must expire at the same time, be of the same type, and have the same underlying contract. Also known as a *Ladder*.

圣诞树涉及三个行权价的价差。以最低（最高）行权价购买一个或多个看涨（看跌），并以每个较高（较低）行权价出售一个或多个看涨（看跌）。所有期权必须同时到期、类型相同且具有相同的标的合约。也被称为梯子。

<!-- source:block 2439729a72967e7c -->

> [!quote]- English 28
> **Class** All options of the same type with the same expiration date and same underlying instrument.

类别相同类型、具有相同到期日期和相同标的工具的所有期权。

<!-- source:block 84b360bccb99747e -->

> [!quote]- English 29
> **Clearinghouse** The organization that guarantees the integrity of all trades made on an exchange.

Clearinghouse保证交易所所有交易完整性的组织。

<!-- source:block d287b50fdf098982 -->

> [!quote]- English 30
> **Clearing Member** A member firm of an exchange that is authorized by the clearinghouse to process trades for its customers and that guarantees, through the collection of margin and variation, the integrity of its customers’ trades.

清算会员交易所的成员公司，经清算所授权为其客户处理交易，并通过收取保证金和变更来保证客户交易的完整性。

<!-- source:block b4595e54eb935c9f -->

> [!quote]- English 31
> **Collar** A long (short) underlying position that is hedged with both a long (short) out-of-the-money put and a short (long) out-of-the-money call. All options must expire at the same time. Also known as a *Cylinder*, *Fence*, or *Range Forward*.

**Collar（领式策略）**：标的多头（空头），同时配以价外看跌期权多头（空头）和价外看涨期权空头（多头）进行对冲。所有期权必须同时到期。亦称 *Cylinder*、*Fence* 或 *Range Forward*。

<!-- source:block 6998e82df15fbc0c -->

> [!quote]- English 32
> **Color** The sensitivity of an option’s gamma to the passage of time.

Color期权的Gamma对时间流逝的敏感度。

<!-- source:block 14436f95e4f238da -->

> [!quote]- English 33
> **Combination (Combo)** A two-sided option spread that does not fall into any well-defined category of spreads. Most commonly, it refers to a long call and short put or short call and long put, which together make up a synthetic position in the underlying contract.

组合（Combo）一种双边期权价差，不属于任何明确定义的价差类别。最常见的是，它指的是多头看涨和空头看跌或空头看涨和多头看跌，它们共同构成了标的合约中的合成头寸。

<!-- source:block 446d752aefa58a24 -->

> [!quote]- English 34
> **Compound Option** An option to purchase an option.

复合期权购买期权的期权。

<!-- source:block 517b08239ab1f8ce -->

> [!quote]- English 35
> **Condor** The sale (purchase) of two options with different exercise prices, together with the purchase (sale) of one option with a lower exercise price and one option with a higher exercise price. All options must be of the same type, have the same underlying contract, and expire at the same time, and there must be an equal increment between exercise prices.

Condor出售（购买）具有不同行权价的两个期权，以及购买（出售）一个行权价较低的期权和一个行权价较高的期权。所有期权必须类型相同，具有相同的标的合约，并且同时到期，并且行权价之间必须相等增量。

<!-- source:block d581a6c484f050cf -->

> [!quote]- English 36
> **Contango** A futures market where the long-term delivery months trade at a premium to the short-term delivery months.

Contango长期交割月份的交易比短期交割月份有溢价的期货市场。

<!-- source:block c6aca050bde96543 -->

> [!quote]- English 37
> **Contingency Order** An order that becomes effective only on the fulfillment of some predetermined condition(s) in the marketplace.

应急订单仅在满足市场上某些预定条件时生效的订单。

<!-- source:block 7f7a4f1d86e12f3e -->

> [!quote]- English 38
> **Conversion** A long underlying position together with a synthetic short underlying position. The synthetic position consists of a short call and long put, where both options have the same exercise price and expire at the same time. Sometimes referred to as a *Forward Conversion*.

转换一个做多的标的头寸加上一个合成的做空标的头寸。合成头寸由看涨期权和看跌期权组成，两种期权具有相同的行权价，并在同一时间到期。有时被称为正向转换。

<!-- source:block 659af17f1d8b6a1d -->

> [!quote]- English 39
> **Covered Write** The sale of a call (put) option against an existing long (short) position in the underlying contract.

**Covered Write（备兑卖出）**：针对已有的标的多头（空头）卖出看涨（看跌）期权。

<!-- source:block dd2d4180bb5e1722 -->

> [!quote]- English 40
> **Cylinder** See *Collar*.

气缸参见衣领。

<!-- source:block 6564277f8a3ef86e -->

> [!quote]- English 41
> **Delta (Δ)** The sensitivity of an option’s theoretical value to a change in the price of the underlying contract. Also known as the *Hedge Ratio*.

Delta（Δ）期权理论价值对标的合约价格变化的敏感性。也称为对冲比率。

<!-- source:block 097bdc4b72da4e89 -->

> [!quote]- English 42
> **Delta Neutral** A position where the sum total of all the deltas add up to approximately 0. Under current market conditions, the position has no preference as to the direction of movement in the underlying market.

Delta Neutral所有Delta总和约为0的头寸。在当前市场条件下，头寸对标的市场的走势方向没有偏好。

<!-- source:block f40b1a1868b61cc9 -->

> [!quote]- English 43
> **Diagonal Roll** See *Time Box*.

对角线滚动参见时间框。

<!-- source:block 8a2a21916c2f9eaf -->

> [!quote]- English 44
> **Diagonal Spread** A long option at one exercise price and expiration date, together with a short option at a different exercise price and expiration date. All options must be the same type. This is the same as a calendar spread using different exercise prices.

对角线价差具有一个行权价和到期日的多头期权，以及具有不同行权价和到期日的空头期权。所有期权必须是相同类型。这与使用不同行权价的日历价差相同。

<!-- source:block a68251e9a9a4bb69 -->

> [!quote]- English 45
> **Digital Option** See *Binary Option*.

数字期权请参阅二元期权。

<!-- source:block b04916daa1440c7e -->

> [!quote]- English 46
> **Dragonfly** A long (short) straddle, together with two short (long) strangles at the same exercise price, where all options expire at the same time and have the same underlying contract. The exercise price of the straddle will usually fall as close as possible to the midpoint between the exercise prices of the strangles.

**Dragonfly（蜻蜓价差）**：一组多头（空头）跨式，加上两组空头（多头）宽跨式；所有期权具有相同标的并同时到期。跨式行权价通常尽可能接近两组宽跨式行权价的中点。

<!-- source:block b30f53f4bcbe2e9f -->

> [!quote]- English 47
> **Dynamic Hedging** A process in which the underlying contract is periodically bought or sold in order to maintain a desired position in a market. Dynamic hedging is most often used to maintain a delta-neutral option position.

动态对冲定期购买或出售标的合约以维持市场所需头寸的过程。动态对冲最常用于维持Delta中性期权头寸。

<!-- source:block dfa91a413d72fb06 -->

> [!quote]- English 48
> **Efficiency** A number that represents the relative risk and reward of a potential option strategy. The risk and reward are typically represented by the total gamma, theta, and vega of the strategy. The efficiency is generated by dividing one sensitivity by another.

效率代表潜在期权策略相对风险和回报的数字。风险和回报通常由策略的总Gamma、Theta和Vega表示。效率是通过将一种灵敏度除以另一种灵敏度来产生的。

<!-- source:block 284bcafae08308eb -->

> [!quote]- English 49
> **Elasticity** The percent change in an option’s value for a given percent change in the value of the underlying instrument. Sometimes referred to as an option’s *Leverage Value*. The elasticity is sometimes denoted by the Greek letter *Lambda* (Λ).

弹性是指标的工具价值每变动一定百分比时，期权价值所发生的百分比变动。有时也称为期权的杠杆值。弹性有时用希腊字母 Lambda（Λ）表示。

<!-- source:block 726dc775800066d6 -->

> [!quote]- English 50
> **Eurocurrency** Currency deposited in a bank outside the currency’s home country.

欧洲货币存放在货币母国以外银行的货币。

<!-- source:block 779aac10eb51d5ab -->

> [!quote]- English 51
> **Eurocurrency rate** The interest rate paid on currency deposited in a bank outside the currency’s home country.

欧洲货币利率存放在货币母国以外银行的货币支付的利率。

<!-- source:block 4b17a77403ff1100 -->

> [!quote]- English 52
> **European Option** An option that may only be exercised at expiration.

欧洲期权只能在到期时行权的期权。

<!-- source:block 53bc55569b33a892 -->

> [!quote]- English 53
> **Exchange Option** An option to exchange one asset for another asset.

交换期权将一种资产交换为另一种资产的期权。

<!-- source:block 67285ff73a480466 -->

> [!quote]- English 54
> **Ex-Dividend** The first day on which a dividend-paying stock is trading without the right to receive the dividend.

除息派息股票交易但无权领取股息的第一天。

<!-- source:block f3eb858744998ae9 -->

> [!quote]- English 55
> **Exercise** The process by which the holder of an option notifies the seller of his intention to take a long position in the underlying contract in the case of a call or a short position in the underlying contract in the case of a put.

行权期权持有人通知卖方他打算在看涨的情况下在标的合约中持有多头头寸的过程，或者在看跌的情况下在标的合约中持有空头头寸的过程。

<!-- source:block f702d5b21891e8d5 -->

> [!quote]- English 56
> **Exercise Price** The price at which the underlying contract will be delivered in the event an option is exercised. Also known as the *Strike Price*.

行权价在期权被行权时，标的合约将被交付的价格。也被称为罢工价格。

<!-- source:block 8cdaaf77ab5d9c22 -->

> [!quote]- English 57
> **Expiration (Expiry)** The date and time after which an option may no longer be exercised.

到期日（Expiry）期权不再行权的日期和时间。

<!-- source:block 5b34187ddee05662 -->

> [!quote]- English 58
> **Exotic Option** An option with nonstandard contract specifications. Sometimes referred to as a *Second-Generation Option*. Exotic options are usually traded in the over-the-counter (off -exchange) market.

异国期权具有非标准合约规格的期权。有时被称为第二代期权。奇异期权通常在场外（场外）市场交易。

<!-- source:block 3b7c002524b59af6 -->

> [!quote]- English 59
> **Extrinsic Value** See *Time Value*.

外部价值请参阅时间价值。

<!-- source:block ef40c03172d40e10 -->

> [!quote]- English 60
> **Fair Value** See *Theoretical Value*.

公允价值请参阅理论价值。

<!-- source:block 9ab95f39196d23a2 -->

> [!quote]- English 61
> **Fence** See *Collar*.

栅栏看到衣领。

<!-- source:block 88d8c6419ba0ac8d -->

> [!quote]- English 62
> **Fill or Kill (FOK)** An order that will automatically be canceled unless it can be executed immediately and in its entirety.

填充或杀死（FOK）除非可以立即完整执行，否则将自动取消的订单。

<!-- source:block 2d823779690a5ab1 -->

> [!quote]- English 63
> **Flex Option** An exchange-traded option where the buyer and seller are permitted to negotiate the exact terms of the option contract. Typically, this includes the exercise price, the expiration date, and the exercise style (either European or American).

弹性期权交易所交易的期权，允许买方和卖方就期权合约的确切条款进行谈判。通常，这包括行权价、到期日和行权风格（欧洲或美国）。

<!-- source:block b5508bd0b15aee87 -->

> [!quote]- English 64
> **Floor** A contract between a borrower and a lender of floating-rate funds whereby the lender is assured of receiving no less than some minimum interest rate for loaned funds. This is analogous to a put option where the underlying instrument is an interest rate on loaned funds.

下限借款人和浮动利率资金贷方之间的合约，贷方保证获得不低于借出资金的某个最低利率。这类似于看跌期权，其中标的工具是贷款资金的利率。
